# PrintSync Motion Language

## Core Principle

**Precision in motion.**

Every movement is intentional, mechanically precise, and serves the story.

## Motion Personalities

### Mechanical
- Production-line cadence
- Rhythmic, precise, engineered
- Think: printing press, assembly line, machine

### Transformative
- Digital → physical
- Flat → dimensional
- Screen → paper
- Idea → object

### Typographic
- Type as character
- Kinetic choreography
- Letters that build, slam, slice, explode, morph

### Cinematic
- Scale and drama
- Camera movement with purpose
- Lighting that creates mood

### Playful
- Unexpected transitions
- Visual jokes
- Scale surprises
- Humour mechanics

## Timing Principles

1. **Intentional pacing** — Never rushed, never dragging
2. **Mechanical rhythm** — Production-line cadence
3. **Impact moments** — Key frames land with precision
4. **Breathing room** — Space between actions
5. **Brand cadence** — Consistent with PrintSync identity

## Transition Language

- Match cuts (shape, color, movement)
- Scale transitions (tiny → enormous)
- Digital → physical transformations
- Typographic choreography
- Geometric transitions
- Light-based transitions

## What to Avoid

- Random easing without purpose
- Overused stock transitions
- Generic AI video aesthetics
- Motion without meaning
- Repetitive movement patterns
- Decorative animation without story
