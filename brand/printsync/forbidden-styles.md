# PrintSync Forbidden Styles

## Absolutely Forbidden

### Generic Corporate
- Generic office workers in meetings
- Handshakes between suits
- Generic stock photography
- "Team collaboration" clichés
- Generic printer imagery (just a printer sitting there)
- Empty buzzword slides

### Template Aesthetics
- Canva-style layouts
- Template-looking social posts
- Generic gradient backgrounds
- Overused stock video aesthetics
- Generic AI corporate video look

### Visual Clichés
- Excessive gradients without purpose
- Generic "quality services" adverts
- Repetitive compositions
- Random visual styles disconnected from brand
- Fake claims or statistics

### AI-Specific
- AI-looking typography (warped, inconsistent)
- Generic AI video aesthetics
- Uncanny human figures
- Inconsistent lighting
- Morphing artifacts
- Generic AI "corporate" look

### Content
- Empty marketing buzzwords
- Fake testimonials
- Invented statistics
- Unsupported claims
- Generic "we provide quality services"
- "Contact us for a quote" without substance

## The Test

Before any creative is approved, ask:

1. Does this look like a template?
2. Does this look like generic AI output?
3. Could this be any brand other than PrintSync?
4. Is there a strong visual idea here?
5. Would a serious creative director approve this?
6. Would a customer remember this?

If the answer to any of these is negative → **REJECT AND RECONCEPTUALIZE**
