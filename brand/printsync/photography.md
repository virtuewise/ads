# PrintSync Photography Direction

## Style

**Technical precision meets creative ambition.**

Every photograph should feel intentional, well-lit, and purposeful.

## Subject Matter

### Primary
- Print objects in context (business cards, posters, signage)
- Production environment (machinery, materials, process)
- Finished installations (signage, displays, branding)
- Material details (paper texture, ink, finish)

### Secondary
- Business environments (not generic offices)
- Brand applications (real-world impact)
- People interacting with print (not stock handshakes)

### Avoid
- Generic stock photography
- Generic office workers
- Handshakes between suits
- Generic "team collaboration"
- Generic printer imagery (just a printer sitting there)

## Technical Quality

- Sharp focus on print details
- Accurate color representation
- Professional lighting
- Clean compositions
- Intentional depth of field

## Color Approach

- True-to-life color accuracy
- PrintSync red as accent when appropriate
- Clean, professional palette
- No excessive filters or grading
