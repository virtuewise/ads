# PrintSync Brand Guide

## Brand Identity

**PrintSync** — Premium printing, branding, signage and visual communication solutions that transform ideas into tangible brand experiences.

## Brand Personality

PrintSync should consistently feel:

- Precise
- Creative
- Intelligent
- Modern
- Reliable
- Ambitious
- Technically capable
- Premium
- Energetic
- Persistent
- Detail-oriented
- Confident

## Brand Pillars

1. **CONSISTENCY** — Every touchpoint, every time
2. **PERSEVERANCE** — We don't stop until it's perfect
3. **CREATIVITY** — We think beyond the brief
4. **PRECISION** — Every detail matters

## Core Messages

- **Primary**: "Your Vision. Our Precision."
- **Secondary**: "Ideas becoming physical impact."
- **Tertiary**: "From screen to something real."

## Visual Language

### Colors
- **Primary Red**: #C8001C — Brand moments, CTAs, highlights
- **Charcoal**: #373435 — Typography, structure, sophistication
- **White**: #FFFFFF — Space, clarity, premium feel

### Typography
- **Primary**: Inter (bold, geometric sans-serif)
- **Secondary**: JetBrains Mono (technical, data, labels)

### Visual Elements
- Technical grids and measurement systems
- Registration marks and crop marks
- Print objects as characters
- Kinetic typography
- Scale transformations
- Digital → physical transitions

## Creative Territories

### TERRITORY 01 — PRECISION
Technical grids. Measurement. Registration marks. Data. Machinery. Precision typography.

### TERRITORY 02 — TRANSFORMATION
Digital → physical. Flat → dimensional. Screen → paper. Idea → object.

### TERRITORY 03 — SCALE
Tiny → enormous. Business card → building. Poster → environment. Sign → architecture.

### TERRITORY 04 — HUMAN IMPACT
Businesses. Customers. Brand experiences. Real-world applications.

### TERRITORY 05 — PLAY
Humour. Surprise. Unexpected scale. Visual jokes. Detective stories.

## Forbidden Styles

- Generic office workers
- Generic handshakes
- Generic stock photography
- Generic printer imagery
- Generic corporate gradients
- Template-looking Canva layouts
- Empty marketing buzzwords
- Repetitive compositions
- AI-looking typography
- Excessive gradients without purpose
- Generic "We provide quality services" adverts
- Template-looking social posts
- Fake claims

## The Test

Before any creative is approved:

1. Does this look like a template?
2. Does this look like generic AI output?
3. Could this be any brand other than PrintSync?
4. Is there a strong visual idea here?
5. Would a serious creative director approve this?
6. Would a customer remember this?

If the answer to any of these is negative → **REJECT AND RECONCEPTUALIZE**
