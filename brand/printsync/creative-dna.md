# PrintSync Creative DNA

## The Creative Rule

**NEVER produce generic corporate advertising.**

Every piece of creative must contain at least one strong visual idea, metaphor, narrative device, unexpected transition, unusual scale relationship, kinetic typography system, graphic information system, humour mechanism, VFX idea, or storytelling device.

## Creative Territories

### TERRITORY 01 — PRECISION
Technical grids. Measurement. Registration marks. Data. Machinery. Precision typography.

### TERRITORY 02 — TRANSFORMATION
Digital → physical. Flat → dimensional. Screen → paper. Idea → object.

### TERRITORY 03 — SCALE
Tiny → enormous. Business card → building. Poster → environment. Sign → architecture.

### TERRITORY 04 — HUMAN IMPACT
Businesses. Customers. Brand experiences. Real-world applications.

### TERRITORY 05 — PLAY
Humour. Surprise. Unexpected scale. Visual jokes. Detective stories.

## Creative Signatures

1. **Kinetic Typography** — Type as character, not decoration
2. **Print Physics** — Physical print objects behaving impossibly
3. **Technical Graphics** — Engineering intelligence made visible
4. **Scale Transitions** — From business card to building
5. **Digital→Physical** — The moment ideas become real

## Anti-Generic Principles

- No generic office workers
- No generic handshakes
- No generic stock photography
- No generic printer imagery
- No generic corporate gradients
- No template-looking Canva layouts
- No empty marketing buzzwords
- No repetitive compositions
- No unnecessary AI-generated text
- No random visual effects
- No generic AI video aesthetics

## Creative Variation Engine

The system must deliberately rotate across:
- **Mood**: cinematic, playful, technical, minimal, dramatic
- **Narrative**: story, detective, educational, humorous, experimental
- **Visual style**: typography, product, VFX, live action, mixed
- **Pace**: slow and premium, fast and energetic, rhythmic
- **Voice**: voiceover, typography-only, music-driven, dialogue, hybrid
