# Brand

PrintSync is the default brand template within Virtue Studio. Virtue Studio is a multi-brand advertising engine that can support any brand; PrintSync is the first/featured brand implementation. The **brand brain** lives in
`brand/printsync/` and is loaded through `@printsync/brand-engine` — the rest of the system should not
read brand files directly.

## Identity

- **Name:** PrintSync
- **Tagline (primary message):** *Your Vision. Our Precision.*
- **Positioning:** Premium printing, branding, signage and visual communication solutions that
  transform ideas into tangible brand experiences.
- **Visual principle:** Ideas becoming physical impact.
- **Motion principle:** Precision in motion.
- **Studio motion rule:** everything Virtue Studio produces is motion-designed — a poster is an
  animated key visual (still frames ship for print); stories and social posts are animated.
- **Creative rule:** **Never look generic.**

## Typography

- **Primary:** Inter (bold, geometric sans-serif)
- **Secondary:** JetBrains Mono (technical, data, labels)
- **Principles:** type is a character; never decorative without purpose; precision over decoration
- **Kinetic behaviours (18):** TYPE-SLAM, TYPE-BUILD, TYPE-SCAN, TYPE-SLICE, TYPE-EXPLODE,
  TYPE-MORPH, TYPE-STRETCH, TYPE-TRACK, TYPE-LOCK, TYPE-REVEAL, TYPE-IMPACT, TYPE-PARTICLE,
  TYPE-MATERIALIZE, TYPE-PRINT, TYPE-CUT, TYPE-FOLD, TYPE-STACK, TYPE-SCALE

## Creative territories

1. **PRECISION** — Technical grids, measurement, registration marks, data, machinery
2. **TRANSFORMATION** — Digital → physical, flat → dimensional, screen → paper, idea → object
3. **SCALE** — Tiny → enormous, business card → building, poster → environment
4. **HUMAN IMPACT** — Businesses, customers, brand experiences, real-world applications
5. **PLAY** — Humour, surprise, unexpected scale, visual jokes, detective stories

## Creative signatures

Kinetic Typography · Print Physics · Technical Graphics · Scale Transitions · Digital to Physical

## Motion language

- **Personalities:** Mechanical, Transformative, Typographic, Cinematic, Playful
- **Timing principles:** intentional pacing, mechanical rhythm, impact moments, breathing room,
  brand cadence
- **Transitions:** match cuts, scale transitions, digital-to-physical transformations, typographic
  choreography, geometric transitions, light-based transitions
- **Avoid:** random easing, overused stock transitions, generic AI video aesthetics, motion without
  meaning, repetitive movement

## Forbidden styles

Generic office workers · generic handshakes · generic stock photography · generic printer imagery ·
generic corporate gradients · template-looking Canva layouts · empty marketing buzzwords · repetitive
compositions · AI-looking typography · excessive gradients without purpose · generic "we provide
quality services" adverts · template social posts · fake claims.

## Logo rules

- File: `assets/logo/printsync-official-logo.svg`
- Clear space: 25% of logo width on all sides
- Minimum size: 120px width (digital), 25mm width (print)
- Colour usage: red on white; white reversed; charcoal monochrome on light
- Never: change colours, add effects, rotate/skew, or place on low-contrast backgrounds

## The test

Before any creative is approved:

1. Does this look like a template?
2. Does this look like generic AI output?
3. Could this be any brand other than PrintSync?
4. Is there a strong visual idea here?
5. Would a serious creative director approve this?
6. Would a customer remember this?

If any answer is negative → **reject and reconceptualize**.
