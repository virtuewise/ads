# PrintSync Logo Rules

## The Logo

The official PrintSync logo is an SVG file located at:
`assets/logo/printsync-official-logo.svg`

## Rules

1. **Never recreate the logo** — Always use the official SVG
2. **Never modify the logo** — No color changes, no effects, no distortions
3. **Never place on busy backgrounds** — Maintain clear space
4. **Never scale below minimum size** — Maintain legibility
5. **Never rotate or skew** — Logo remains upright
6. **Never add effects** — No shadows, glows, or 3D effects
7. **Always maintain aspect ratio** — No stretching

## Clear Space

Minimum clear space = 25% of logo width on all sides.

## Minimum Size

- Digital: 120px width minimum
- Print: 25mm width minimum

## Color Usage

- **Primary**: Red (#C8001C) logo on white background
- **Reversed**: White logo on dark/charcoal background
- **Monochrome**: Charcoal logo on light background (single color only)

## Incorrect Usage

- Changing logo colors
- Adding drop shadows or glows
- Rotating or skewing
- Placing on low-contrast backgrounds
- Adding effects or filters
- Recreating or redrawing
- Stretching or distorting
